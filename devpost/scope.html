🦥 LazyLegend

An anti-productivity tracker rewarding unproductivity with deadpan corporate praise.

🎯 The Unique Kernel

Deadpan inversion of serious productivity dashboards: Taking underachievement with absolute corporate seriousness. Measuring idle time, tasks avoided, and excuses invented, converting pure downtime into an absurdly competitive sport.

Design principle: 3 seconds to understand, 10 seconds to smile, 30 seconds to produce something funny to send to a friend.

🔄 The Core Loop Architecture

How a friend experiences the satirical loop from entry to viral share:

📦 Proof of Concept Boundary

  • Daily Laziness Dashboard Live Laziness Score, sleepy sloth corporate greeting, and key underachievement stats.
  • Sloth Mode Inactivity Timer Active in-browser timer rewarding minutes of pure, uninterrupted stillness.
  • Procrastination Confessional & Excuse Generator Quick-log avoided tasks or roll instant hilarious corporate excuses for slacking.
  • Absurd Badge System Instant celebratory confetti upon unlocking badges like "Supreme Couch Commander" and "Tomorrow's Problem".
  • Official Slacker Certificate 1-click formatted clipboard export ("LAZYLEGEND • A+ Outstanding Underachievement").
  • Local Mock Friends Leaderboard Pre-seeded fictional slackers competing for top underachievement honours.
  • Real-time Multi-user Group Sync Live backend syncing so actual friend circles can compete on a shared leaderboard.
  • Native OS Screen-time & Inactivity Hooks Platform background telemetry with explicit user permissions.
  • Exit Push Notifications "Too much effort? Understandable. +50 points awarded."
  • Weekly Slacker Oscar Ceremony Cinematic animated weekly awards for low effort.
  • Invasive Background Surveillance No covert keylogging or activity stalking—keeps it lighthearted and self-reported.
  • Mandatory Account Auth & Login Walls Avoids killing the 30-second time-to-delight.
  • Real Productivity Integrations (Jira, Google Calendar) Defeats the satirical, feel-good soul of the app.
