# LazyLegend

A delightfully ridiculous, feel-good web app that poses as a serious corporate productivity tracker, except its entire purpose is to celebrate and reward unproductivity.

## The Unique Kernel
Deadpan inversion of serious productivity trackers: measuring idle time, tasks avoided, and excuses invented with enterprise-grade charts and sincere corporate praise, turning doing nothing into an absurdly competitive, shareable sport.

## Who It's For
Friends and colleagues inundated with relentless AI productivity hype who want a laugh, a counter-cultural breather, and a way to brag about being a gloriously accomplished slacker.

## The Core Loop
1. Open the dashboard and get greeted by the sleepy sloth with deadpan praise ("Inactive for 47 minutes. That's commitment.").
2. Enter "Sloth Mode" to let the inactivity timer tick up, or log a postponed chore / generate an absurd excuse.
3. Watch the Laziness Score climb, confetti burst, and badges unlock ("Supreme Couch Commander", "Tomorrow's Problem").
4. Hit "Copy Shareable Report" to generate an official A+ Slacker Certificate to paste into group chats.

## Inspiration & Identity
- **Tone:** Deadpan corporate seriousness meets playful absurdity. High praise for zero effort.
- **Visual Feel:** Clean, modern productivity dashboard (resembling Linear, Notion, or Stripe) with polished cards, subtle dark mode / pastel accents, and an adorable sleepy sloth mascot.
- **Principle:** 3 seconds to understand, 10 seconds to smile, 30 seconds to produce something funny to send to a friend.

## Why This Matters to the Learner
A fun, personal antidote to ubiquitous AI productivity discussions, built to entertain friends while mastering how to steer an AI coding agent to a precisely defined outcome.

## What "Working" Looks Like
A crisp, 60-second browser walkthrough:
1. Open the app to see today's Laziness Score (e.g., 847 / 1,000, Elite Slacker).
2. Start the Sloth Mode inactivity timer and watch it reward standing still.
3. Confess an avoided task or click "Generate Excuse" to boost the score and pop celebratory confetti.
4. Unlock an achievement badge and copy the formatted "Official Slacker Certificate" to clipboard with one click.

## The POC Boundary
- **Daily Laziness Dashboard:** Today's score, sloth greeting, and key stats (Idle time, Tasks avoided, Excuses logged).
- **Sloth Mode Timer:** Active in-browser inactivity counter with live encouragement.
- **Procrastination Confessional:** Quick-log avoided tasks or generate random hilarious excuses.
- **Badge Unlocks:** Instant gratification confetti and badges ("Supreme Couch Commander", "Speed Is Overrated").
- **Shareable Certificate:** 1-click clipboard copy of the A+ Slacker Report Card.
- **Mock Friends Leaderboard:** Pre-populated with funny fictional slackers (e.g., "Dave: Canceled zoom call to take a nap").

## Later
- Multi-user live backend syncing for real friends.
- OS-level screen time / inactivity background tracking.
- Web push notifications when you exit the app ("Too much effort? Understandable. +50 points").
- Weekly Slacker Awards Ceremony with Oscar-style animated presentations.

## Explicitly Cut
- Invasive system surveillance or background monitoring (violates privacy and ruins the lighthearted, self-reported spirit).
- Account creation / login friction (slows down the 30-second time-to-value).
- Real productivity integrations like Google Calendar or Jira (counter to the entire comedic premise).
